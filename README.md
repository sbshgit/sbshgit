## 👋 About Me

I’m **Saba Shiranirad**, a PharmD and PhD candidate in **Artificial Intelligence in Medical Sciences**.

My research lies at the intersection of **pharmaceutical sciences, artificial intelligence, and computational drug discovery**, with a particular interest in:

- 🧬 Molecular representation learning
- 🕸️ Graph neural networks and multimodal learning
- 💊 Drug discovery and virtual screening
- 🔬 AI-driven pharmaceutical research
- 🧠 Explainable AI for biomedical applications

My goal is to develop **interpretable AI methods that can accelerate drug discovery and pharmaceutical research**.


## 🔬 Research Interests

- Artificial Intelligence in Drug Discovery
- Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)
- Molecular Representation Learning
- Virtual Screening
- Explainable AI (XAI)
- Cheminformatics
- Computational Pharmacology
- AI-driven Pharmaceutical Sciences
